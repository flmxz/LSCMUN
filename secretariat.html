Letter from the Secretary-General

Distinguished Delegates, Faculty Advisors, and Guests,

It is my distinct honor to welcome you to the official portal of LSC Model United Nations 2026. Over the past year, our team has poured endless effort into curating an immersive, academically rigorous environment designed to test your diplomatic skills, strategic reasoning, and policy acumen.

Whether you are stepping into a committee room for the first time or leading advanced debate in a specialized crisis cabinet, LSCMUN promises an unprecedented experience. We look forward to witnessing your constructive solutions and fierce debate this conference year.

Alexander Wright Secretary-General, LSCMUN '26
