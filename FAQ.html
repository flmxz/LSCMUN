Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding delegate participation, rules, accommodations, and conference policies.

What is the deadline for delegate registration? +
Early bird registrations close 45 days prior to Day 1. Regular registration closes 14 days prior to the conference. We recommend applying early to secure preferred committee allocations.
Can I apply as an independent delegate without a school delegation? +
Yes! We welcome both school/university delegations and independent delegates. Simply select "Independent Delegate" on the registration form.
Are double delegations allowed in all committees? +
Double delegations are permitted exclusively in specified councils, such as the UN Security Council (UNSC). All other general assembly committees are single delegation.
How are awards evaluated during the conference? +
Awards (Best Delegate, Outstanding Delegate, Honorable Mention, Best Position Paper) are judged on constructive debate, accuracy of country policy, parliamentary procedure, resolution authorship, and diplomatic negotiation.
Are position papers mandatory? +
Position papers are required for delegates who wish to be eligible for Best Position Paper and Best Delegate awards. They must be submitted via the Resources portal prior to the deadline.
What is the required dress code for committee sessions? +
Western Business Attire (WBA) or formal national traditional wear is required during all committee sessions. Casual clothing (jeans, sneakers, t-shirts) is strictly prohibited during proceedings.
Is accommodation provided for international delegates? +
While LSCMUN does not directly provide housing, we partner with local hotels to offer discounted room rates for out-of-town delegates. Check the Contact page or email delegate affairs for partner codes.

Still have unanswered questions?

Our Secretariat team is here to assist you with any specific queries or custom requests.
