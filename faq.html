Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about delegate registration, logistics, and conference policies.

Who is eligible to participate in LSCMUN '26? +
LSCMUN '26 is open to undergraduate and postgraduate students from all recognized academic institutions and disciplines. High school delegates from 11th and 12th grades are also eligible upon institutional verification.
Do I need prior MUN experience or a science background? +
No prior Model UN experience or specialized background in life sciences is required. Detailed study guides, rulebooks, and orientation workshops are provided to all registered delegates prior to the conference.
How are committee allocations assigned? +
Allocations are processed on a first-come, first-served basis according to preferences indicated during registration. Matrix assignments are sent via email within 5-7 business days after payment confirmation.
What is the dress code for the conference? +
Delegates must adhere to Western Formal attire (suits, blazers, formal shirts, or formal dresses) or Traditional Ethnic Formal wear during all official committee sessions.
Are accommodation and meals included in the delegate fee? +
Delegate fees include official delegate kits, high tea, and lunch for both days of the conference. Accommodation is not included directly, but partner hotel packages near the campus are available upon request.
What is the refund and cancellation policy? +
Delegate registration fees are strictly non-refundable. However, delegate substitutions are permitted up to 10 days prior to the conference upon written notification to the Secretariat.
