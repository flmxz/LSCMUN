Terms & Code of Conduct

Conference guidelines, delegate behavioral expectations, and administrative terms of service.

01. Delegate Code of Conduct

LSCMUN is committed to creating a respectful, diplomatic, and inclusive environment. All delegates, faculty advisors, and observers are required to adhere to the following standards:

  • Diplomatic Decorum: Delegates must treat Dais members, conference staff, and fellow participants with mutual respect at all times. Personal attacks or derogatory language will result in immediate disciplinary action.
  • Dress Code: Western Business Attire (WBA) or formal traditional national dress is mandatory during all committee sessions.
  • Substance Policy: The possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes, or illicit substances on conference premises is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate expulsion.

02. Academic Integrity & Plagiarism

LSCMUN upholds strict academic standards regarding delegate work and submissions:

  • Original Work: All position papers, working papers, and draft resolutions must be the original work of the submitting delegate or delegation bloc.
  • Plagiarism & AI Policy: Unattributed copying, pre-written resolutions drafted prior to the conference, or uncredited AI-generated text will lead to immediate disqualification from award eligibility.

03. Registration & Fee Policies

Terms governing conference registration, matrix allocations, and cancellations:

  • Country Allotments: Committee and country assignments are allocated at the discretion of the Secretariat based on experience and submission timeliness.
  • Refunds & Transfers: Registration fees are non-refundable after the official registration deadline. Delegation transfers must be requested in writing at least 14 days prior to Day 1.

04. Media Release & Privacy

By participating in LSCMUN '26, delegates grant permission for media coverage:

  • Photography & Video: Official media staff will photograph and record committee proceedings for promotional and archival use on official channels.
  • Data Privacy: Delegate contact details provided during registration are strictly used for conference administration and will not be shared with third parties without prior consent.
