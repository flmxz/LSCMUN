Frequently Asked Questions

What is LSCMUN '26? +
LSCMUN is an academic Model United Nations simulation organized by the Department of Life Sciences at Christ University Central Campus, Bangalore, linking scientific knowledge with real-world diplomacy and SDGs.
Who can participate as a delegate? +
Students from undergraduate, postgraduate, and high school institutions are eligible to apply as delegates, chairs, security, runners, or press.
What is the Early Bird vs. Late Registration fee? +
Early Bird fee for Delegates/Chairs is ₹699–799 (Late: ₹999). Early Bird for Security, Runners, and Press is ₹499–599 (Late: ₹799–899).
Where is the venue located? +
The conference is hosted at Christ University Central Campus, Hosur Road, Bangalore, Karnataka.
What are the award categories in each council? +
Awards include Best Delegate, Best Speaker, Best Position Paper, and Most Diplomatic across all simulation councils.
What is the dress code? +
Western Business Attire (WBA) or formal traditional wear is required during all council sessions.
Is prior MUN experience necessary? +
No prior experience is necessary. Comprehensive Rules of Procedure (ROP) and delegate training sessions will be provided by Executive Chairs and the R&D team.
Are food and refreshments provided? +
Yes, registration covers in-council breakfast, lunch catering (vegetarian & non-vegetarian options), and afternoon snack breaks on both days.
How can I contact the organizing committee? +
You can reach out via official email at lscmun.26@gmail.com or through the Contact page inquiry form.
