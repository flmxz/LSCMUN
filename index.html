Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate?

Delegates from high schools, colleges, and university institutions are eligible to apply.

What is the dress code?

Western Formal / Business Attire is strictly enforced during all committee sessions.

Are individual delegates allowed?

Yes, both independent delegates and school/college delegations are welcome.

Register for LSCMUN

Applications are open for individual delegates and institutional delegations.
