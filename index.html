Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate in LSCMUN? +
LSCMUN welcomes high school, college, and undergraduate university students. Independent delegates as well as school delegations can apply.
What is the required dress code? +
Western Business Attire (suits, blazers, formal shirts, ties, formal trousers/dresses) is strictly enforced throughout all committee sessions.
When will committee study guides be released? +
Comprehensive Background Guides and Rules of Procedure (ROP) will be emailed to all registered delegates 30 days prior to the conference.
